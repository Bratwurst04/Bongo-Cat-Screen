Interface and implementation research
-------------------------------------
The BONGO DJ screen and architecture were independently adapted for this
ESP32-024 hardware after studying these MIT-licensed projects:

- joeycatic/esp32-spotify-touch-controller
  https://github.com/joeycatic/esp32-spotify-touch-controller
- witnessmenow/Spotify-Diy-Thing
  https://github.com/witnessmenow/Spotify-Diy-Thing
- Saqoosha/SPNFY
  https://github.com/Saqoosha/SPNFY

No credentials or code from those repositories are embedded in the firmware.
Their design patterns informed the cover-first layout, touch gestures, and
separation between media acquisition and display rendering.
